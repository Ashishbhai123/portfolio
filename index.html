About Me

My Web3 journey began in 2022, when a friend introduced me to blockchain and decentralized technologies. What started as curiosity soon became a passion for community building, education, and helping users navigate the Web3 ecosystem.

2022

Community Ambassador @ Humanity Protocol

My first role in Web3, where I gained hands-on experience in community moderation, user support, campaign management, Discord engagement, and educational initiatives. This experience taught me the importance of building welcoming and active communities.

2023

Regional Moderator @ GAIB

Hosted community events, AMAs and game nights, answered user queries, managed reports, and worked closely with the team to strengthen community engagement around AI infrastructure and decentralized finance.

2023 – 2024

Contributor @ ImpossibleCloud, Ping Network, P2P.me

Focused on community growth, educational content, user onboarding, and ecosystem awareness. Through P2P.me, I also helped onboard merchants and users into crypto payment solutions, gaining valuable experience in real-world Web3 adoption.

2024 – Present

Ecosystem Contributor @ Soneium

Active contributor to the Soneium ecosystem, helping grow Startale India by creating educational graphics, banners, and ecosystem content, and engaging with users to make Soneium more accessible to newcomers.

Beyond community management, I continuously expand my technical skills by learning Discord server management, community automation, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub, Google Sheets, and basic web development. My mission is simple: educate, onboard, and empower more people to explore Web3 through meaningful content, active community engagement, and long-term ecosystem growth.
